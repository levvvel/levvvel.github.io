This is the gross value of the block subsidy only, before costs and taxes. Transaction fees vary. It is not a forecast, income estimate, or promise that a LexoMiner user will find a block.
Important: Finding a Bitcoin block is extremely difficult and never guaranteed. A solo miner only receives the block subsidy and transaction fees if their valid block is successfully found and accepted by the network. The probability depends on the miner’s hash rate relative to the entire Bitcoin network. Electricity, hardware, pool/relay, and other costs can exceed any potential reward.
