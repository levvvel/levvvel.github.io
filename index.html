The solo mining idea

One computer. One independent attempt. The Bitcoin network.

Bitcoin miners compete to discover the next valid block. With solo mining, you make that attempt independently: if your miner finds a valid block and the network accepts it, the block subsidy and included transaction fees go to you. Every hash is another attempt, while your odds depend on your computing power compared with the entire network.

Meet xMiNeR

xMiNeR

Windows desktop software

xMiNeR is a Windows application built around the concept of Bitcoin solo mining. Explore the application and its mining-related features.

Important: Bitcoin solo mining gives you an independent chance to discover a block. If your xMiNeR successfully finds a valid block and the Bitcoin network accepts it, you receive the block reward — currently 3.125 BTC — plus the transaction fees included in that block. Every hash is another attempt, and your chances depend on your computing power compared to the total network hashrate. Finding a block is not guaranteed, but the possibility is there.
